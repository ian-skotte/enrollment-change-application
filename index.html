SQE Enrollment Changes — Deadlines, Effective Dates & Documentation

1. Loss of Eligibility

Loss of eligibility under another group plan for any reason (divorce, death of spouse, involuntary loss of other government coverage). Only the employee and dependents who lost eligibility may enroll, and only in the coverage type lost (medical/medical, dental/dental, vision/vision). A voluntary loss of coverage is not a qualifying event.

  • Deadline: received by ABC/BA within 60 days of the loss of eligibility.
  • Effective date: first day of the month after a complete application is received.
  • Documentation: employer/insurer letterhead listing covered participants, coverage dates/types, who lost eligibility, the date, and the reason.

2. Acquisition of New Dependent

Spouse or stepchild by marriage: employee may enroll in employee-only or family coverage, and add the new dependent plus other still-eligible dependents not previously enrolled. Transfer to a different carrier/option is allowed if adding to existing coverage.

By order of guardianship: no employee-only coverage; the request must correspond with the guardianship order's terms.

By birth, adoption, or placement for adoption: submit within 30 days for the earliest effective date. Employee may enroll in employee-only or family coverage and add the new dependent plus other still-eligible dependents.

  • Deadline: received within 60 days of acquisition (30 days for retroactive health coverage on birth/adoption/placement).
  • Effective date: first of the month after receipt, except birth/adoption/placement health coverage may be retroactive to the event date if submitted within 30 days; dental/vision are never retroactive.
  • Documentation: marriage certificate; or birth certificate (mother's copy accepted for newborn); or final adoption/custody order; or guardianship order.

Example — marriage or birth/adoption on June 15: submitted within 30 days (June 25) → coverage begins July 1 (health retroactive to June 15 for birth/adoption); submitted 31–60 days later (August 14) → coverage begins September 1; submitted on/after day 61 (August 15) → denied.

3. New Eligibility for Premium Subsidy

An employee and dependents newly eligible for a CHIP or Medicaid premium subsidy may enroll midyear. The application with documentation must be received by ABC/BA within 60 days of the new eligibility.

Required documentation for adding new dependents may be submitted up to 10 days after the applicable enrollment deadline.
